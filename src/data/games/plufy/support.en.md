# Plufy's World — Support

_Plufy's World is a storybook game for families: walk Plufy and Pobi around the meadow with your finger, then open the three books on the picnic blanket to mix colors, match shadows and trace numbers. Every finished page brings the book's story to life._

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I play?

- **Meadow:** After the opening, Plufy comes to the meadow. Drag your finger from the character: it walks. Swipe down on the character: it sits; swipe up: it jumps; swipe from left to right: it dances; tap the character: it waves. Tapping an empty spot on the meadow also walks the character there. On the first launch a finger icon shows the moves one by one.
- **Pobi:** Tap the **Pobi** button (Pobi's face) in the top-right corner; Pobi comes over and can do the same moves.
- **Books:** Once Pobi has arrived, a **Books** button (a book icon) appears in the same corner. Tap it: Plufy and Pobi walk to the picnic blanket and the picnic screen with the three books opens.
- **Three books:** Tap a book, then tap **PLAY** on the page.
  - _Play with Plufy: Find Colors:_ tap the jars, stir the dough balls in the bowl by drawing circles with your finger and make the target color; then roll the dough out, cut it with the mold and add its eyes and details.
  - _Play with Plufy: Find Shapes:_ drag each piece from the tray onto its own shadow.
  - _Play with Plufy: Play Numbers:_ trace the line or number with your finger from start to end.
- Each book has 20 pages; pages open one after another as you finish them. The ↻ button on a finished page plays it again.

### How do I open Settings? (Parental gate)

Settings and external links are behind the parental gate on the picnic screen:

1. Open the app; the opening screen moves on by itself after a few seconds (or tap to skip) and the meadow opens.
2. On the meadow, tap the **Pobi** button in the top-right corner; when Pobi arrives, tap the **Books** button that appears in the same corner.
3. Plufy and Pobi walk to the picnic blanket and the picnic screen with the three books opens.
4. On the picnic screen, **press and hold the gear icon (⚙︎) in the top-right corner for 1.5 seconds**. A ring fills around the icon; when it is full, Settings opens.

A short tap does not open Settings, so reset and external links cannot be reached by accident. There is no Settings button inside the books or on the meadow; from a book, the back button in the top-left corner returns to the picnic screen.

### Why are there ads?

Plufy's World is free and supported by ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob; every ad request is made for non-personalized ads, with Google's child-directed treatment tag and the strictest content rating (G, general audiences). The app does not request the advertising identifier and never asks for tracking permission. Ads never appear during play; they are shown only after a book page is finished, on the way back to the book. The first 3 games are ad-free; after that one ad every 3 finished games (the first ad appears at the 6th finished game) and no more than 6 per session. There are no ads on the meadow, on the picnic screen or in Settings. Without internet, no ads are shown. Details: [privacy policy](https://huseyindol.com/games/plufy/privacy-policy).

### How do I reset progress?

Settings → **Reset everything** → confirm. Stars, progress in all three books, the tutorial and settings are deleted and the game starts as if newly installed. This cannot be undone.

### How do I turn off sound, music or read-aloud?

- In Settings, **Sound effects**, **Music**, **Vibration** and **Read aloud** can each be turned on or off.
- While playing in the colors book, open the **≡** menu in the top-left corner: the speaker button mutes sound and music together, and the book button returns to the book.
- The game follows the iPhone's silent switch: when the phone is on silent, sounds and read-aloud are not heard.

### How do I change the language?

Settings → **Language**: System / Türkçe / English. With "System", the game opens in Turkish if the phone's language is Turkish, otherwise in English.

### Read-aloud does not work

Read-aloud reads the color and object name on a finished page in the colors book. Check that Read aloud is on in Settings and the phone is not on silent. Read-aloud uses the iPhone's built-in voice; if no voice is installed for the selected language it stays quiet. You can download voices in iPhone Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices.

### Does the game need the internet?

No. The game plays offline. The internet is used only for ads and the privacy policy / support links.
