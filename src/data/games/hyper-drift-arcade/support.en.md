# Hyper Drift: Arcade — Support

_Hyper Drift: Arcade is a top-down 2D drift game: drift through a cone-marked course and collect points; the longer you hold the drift, the higher your combo multiplier climbs, and near misses and drift zones earn bonuses._

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I play? What are the controls?

- **Bottom right — GAS**, **bottom left — BRAKE and HANDBRAKE**: the classic drift setup. Brake into the corner with the handbrake, keep the slide alive with gas.
- **Steering** is chosen in Settings: **Arrow buttons** (hold to steer, springs back on release) or **Steering wheel** (turn it with your finger).
- **Keyboard** (web and desktop): ← → / A D steer, ↑ / W gas, ↓ / S brake and reverse, Space handbrake, Esc pause.
- **Gas lock:** while holding GAS, slide your finger up; gas locks on and stays on until you touch a pedal again (in normal mode, braking also releases the lock).

### How does scoring work?

- Points = **slide angle × speed**. The combo multiplier climbs up to **x8** the longer you drift; a new drift within 1.3 s continues your combo.
- **Bonuses:** a very close pass by a cone is **+60**; red-and-white ringed drift zones give a **x2 / x3** multiplier.
- **Penalties:** hitting a cone costs **−150** points and breaks the combo; a spin also breaks it. Off the track, points drop (parking lot x0.5, grass x0.25).

### What is child mode?

In child mode there is only the **GAS** pedal: the car follows the course on its own, drifts through the corners by itself and does 2 full laps in the drift zones; there are no penalties. With Settings → Child Mode → **Show steering wheel** enabled, a weakened (28%) steering wheel appears: the child sees themselves turning it, but the car never leaves the course.

### Where are sound, player name and the other settings?

- **Settings:** player name (up to 16 characters), steering mode, sound on/off + volume, child mode, keyboard keys and the scoring rules.
- **Garage:** car selection, day/night and effect mode (fireworks, confetti or falling stars while drifting). At night, street lamps and headlights light the course.
- Scores are stored on your device: the top 10 for every course and mode (normal / child).

### Why are there ads?

Hyper Drift: Arcade is free and supported by ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob; every ad request is made for non-personalized ads, with Google's child-directed treatment tag and the strictest content rating (G, general audiences). The app does not request the advertising identifier and never asks for tracking permission. Ads never appear while driving; they are shown only after a run is finished. The first 3 runs are ad-free; after that one ad every 3 finished runs (the first ad appears at the 6th finished run) and no more than 6 per session. There are no rewarded ads, no banners and no in-app purchases. Without internet, no ads are shown. Details: [privacy policy](https://huseyindol.com/games/hyper-drift-arcade/privacy-policy).

### Does the game need the internet?

No. The game plays fully offline. The internet is used only for ads and the privacy policy / support links.
